    <h1>Easy NAS Player</h1>
    <p>Photos and videos from your Synology NAS, on your LG TV</p>
  </div>
</section>

Easy NAS Player lets you view the photos and videos stored on your Synology NAS on your LG webOS TV, using just the remote. Open your NAS folders as they are — no copying or moving files. It is free, has no ads or in-app purchases, and sends no information to the developer.

<ul class="cards">
  <li><strong>Multiple NAS profiles</strong>Connect by address (IP or domain) or QuickConnect ID. Works with a NAS that uses a self-signed certificate.</li>
  <li><strong>Two-step verification</strong>Enter a code, choose "Trust this TV" to skip it next time, or add your authenticator key to fill codes automatically.</li>
  <li><strong>Folder browsing</strong>Photos and videos only, sorted by name, modified or created date. Grid or list view, recent folders, folder covers (cover.jpg / folder.jpg).</li>
  <li><strong>Photo viewer</strong>Full screen, previous / next, zoom in / out, slideshow with an adjustable interval.</li>
  <li><strong>Video playback</strong>Play files directly or stream through Video Station (converted on the NAS), resume where you left off, auto-play the next video.</li>
  <li><strong>Subtitles</strong>Finds .srt, .smi and .vtt files with the same name; adjust size, color, outline and background.</li>
  <li><strong>5 design themes</strong>Midnight Screen, Silver Gallery, Velvet Cinema, Emerald Lounge and Darkroom, each in light and dark.</li>
  <li><strong>Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified)</strong>Follows your TV's language.</li>
</ul>

**Requirements**: a Synology NAS running DSM and an account that can use File Station. Video Station streaming requires Video Station on the NAS.
